Marionette Privacy Policy

Fatui Bot Arsenal
Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Marionette, a Discord application operated as part of the Fatui Bot Arsenal, accesses and processes information when users interact with the application.

1. Information Marionette Accesses

Marionette accesses information provided by Discord that is necessary for its features to function. Depending on how Marionette is used, this may include:

  • Discord user IDs.
  • Discord usernames and server display names.
  • Message content.
  • Server and channel information required for operation.
  • Message attachment and sticker information.
  • Information about mentions and emojis contained in messages.
  • Voice-channel information required for voice functionality.

2. How Marionette Uses Information

Marionette uses this information to provide its text-to-speech and Discord voice functionality.

Message content is processed so that Marionette can convert eligible messages into speech and play the resulting audio in a Discord voice channel.

Marionette may use a user's Discord display name or username and user identifier to determine when a new person begins speaking and announce the speaker when appropriate.

3. Message Processing

Marionette processes message content and certain message metadata to determine what should be spoken.

Mentions may be converted into the mentioned user's display name. Custom emojis may be converted into their names. Links, attachments, and stickers may instead be converted into brief spoken descriptions such as "sent a link", "sent an image", "sent a file", or "sent a sticker."

Marionette does not intentionally download or transmit the contents of images, videos, or other attached files to its text-to-speech engines merely because they are attached to a message.

4. Text-to-Speech Processing

Marionette converts eligible Discord messages into spoken audio using a selected text-to-speech engine. Marionette currently supports Piper and Fish Audio.

Piper

Piper is a locally operated text-to-speech engine. When Piper is selected, Marionette sends the prepared speech text to a Piper process running on the computer hosting Marionette.

Piper performs speech generation locally using a voice model installed on that computer. The text processed by Piper is not sent to an external text-to-speech provider by Marionette.

The resulting audio is returned to Marionette and temporarily stored locally so that it can be played in the appropriate Discord voice channel.

Fish Audio

Fish Audio is an external text-to-speech service. When Fish Audio is selected, Marionette sends the prepared speech text to the Fish Audio API for speech generation.

The request sent by Marionette contains the text to be spoken, the identifier of the selected Fish Audio voice/model, and the requested audio format.

Marionette currently uses a Fish Audio voice model configured for the Sandrone voice.

Fish Audio generates the requested speech and returns the resulting audio to Marionette. The returned audio is temporarily stored on the computer running Marionette while it is prepared for playback.

Fish Audio is an independent third-party service. Information submitted to Fish Audio may be processed and retained according to Fish Audio's own policies and terms. Marionette does not control Fish Audio's internal processing or retention practices.

Users should review Fish Audio's Privacy Policy for information about how Fish Audio handles information submitted to its services.

5. Temporary Processing and Storage

Marionette may temporarily hold message information in memory while processing a message and managing its text-to-speech queue.

Generated audio may be temporarily stored as an audio file on the computer running Marionette while the audio is being prepared or played.

Temporary generated audio files are deleted when they are no longer required by the TTS system.

6. Operational Logs

Marionette may output limited operational information to the console of the computer running the application. This may temporarily include information such as usernames, message content being processed, TTS status, and error information.

These logs are used for monitoring, troubleshooting, and maintaining Marionette. Marionette does not intentionally transmit these operational logs to a separate logging or analytics service.

6. Local Configuration

Marionette stores limited configuration information locally on the computer running the application. This currently includes the selected text-to-speech engine.

This configuration does not constitute a database of users' Discord messages or conversations.

7. Data Retention

Marionette is not designed to maintain a permanent database of Discord messages or conversation history.

Information temporarily processed in memory is discarded as part of normal operation when it is no longer required.

Temporary audio files generated for playback are deleted after they are no longer required by the application.

Data transmitted to third-party services, including Fish Audio, may be subject to those services' own retention and processing policies.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent users' personal information.

Marionette does not intentionally share Discord message content with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

When Fish Audio is selected as the text-to-speech engine, the prepared text that Marionette is instructed to speak is transmitted to Fish Audio so that the requested speech can be generated.

9. Discord

Marionette operates through Discord and uses Discord's APIs and services. Discord independently processes information according to its own policies.

For more information about how Discord handles information, please review the Discord Privacy Policy .

10. Your Choices

Users who do not want their messages processed by Marionette may avoid interacting with the application or request that a server administrator remove Marionette from the server.

Server administrators control whether Marionette is installed in their servers and can remove the application when appropriate.

11. Data Requests

If you have questions about Marionette's handling of information or believe that information associated with you has been retained and would like to request its deletion, please contact us using the address below.
