Privacy Policy

Fatui Bot Arsenal

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Discord applications operated under the Fatui Bot Arsenal handle information when users interact with them.

The Fatui Bot Arsenal is a collection of independently developed Discord applications, including Marionette and future applications that may be added to the Arsenal.

1. Information We Access

Our applications may access information made available to them by Discord when they are installed in a server and when users interact with them.

Depending on the application and its functionality, this may include:

  • Discord usernames and user identifiers.
  • Messages and message content sent in channels where the application has access.
  • Information about attachments, links, emojis, stickers, and other message content.
  • Server and channel information required for the application's operation.
  • Voice-channel information required for voice-related features.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed by our applications is used only to provide, operate, maintain, and improve the functionality of the relevant Discord application.

For example, Marionette accesses message content so that it can convert messages into speech and play the resulting audio in a Discord voice channel.

We do not use messages received by Marionette to build a permanent database of users' conversations.

3. Text-to-Speech Processing

Marionette may use third-party text-to-speech services to generate audio from message content.

When Fish Audio is selected as Marionette's TTS engine, the text that Marionette is instructed to speak is sent to Fish Audio's API for speech generation.

Fish Audio processes requests according to its own terms and privacy policy. Users should review Fish Audio's privacy documentation for information about how Fish Audio handles information submitted to its services.

4. Storage and Retention

We do not intentionally maintain a permanent database of Discord messages received by Marionette.

Audio generated for playback may be temporarily stored on the computer running the application while it is being prepared or played. These temporary audio files are deleted after they are no longer required.

Configuration information required for the operation of an application may be stored locally by the application.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent users' personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services when necessary for an application's functionality. For example, Marionette may transmit text to Fish Audio when Fish Audio is selected as the TTS engine.

We do not intentionally share Discord message content with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

6. Discord

Our applications operate through Discord and are subject to Discord's platform and API policies.

Discord independently processes information according to its own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

You can review Discord's Privacy Policy at Discord Privacy Policy.

7. Your Choices

If you do not want an application to process your messages, you should avoid interacting with the application or ask a server administrator to remove it from the server.

Server administrators control whether our applications are installed in their servers.

8. Data Requests and Deletion

If you believe that the Fatui Bot Arsenal has retained personal information about you and would like to request information about it or request its deletion, you may contact us using the contact information provided below.

9. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

Users should also comply with Discord's applicable age requirements and policies when using Discord and Discord applications.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Fatui Bot Arsenal adds new applications, changes how existing applications process information, or when applicable requirements change.

The date at the beginning of this policy will be updated when material changes are made.
