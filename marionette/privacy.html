Marionette Privacy Policy

Fatui Bot Arsenal

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Marionette, a Discord application operated as part of the Fatui Bot Arsenal, accesses, processes, and handles information when users interact with the application.

1. Information Marionette Accesses

Marionette may access information provided to it by Discord when the application is installed in a server and when users interact with its features.

Depending on how Marionette is configured and used, this may include:

  • Discord usernames and user identifiers.
  • Server and channel identifiers required for operation.
  • Message content that Marionette is permitted to access.
  • Voice-channel information required for voice functionality.
  • Other message content required by Marionette's message-processing features.

2. How Marionette Uses This Information

Marionette accesses information only as necessary to provide its features and operate the application.

In particular, Marionette processes eligible Discord messages so that their content can be converted into speech and played in a Discord voice channel.

3. Message Processing

Marionette does not intentionally maintain a permanent database of users' Discord conversations.

Message content may be temporarily held in memory or in the application's processing queue while Marionette prepares or plays speech.

4. Text-to-Speech Processing

Marionette supports multiple text-to-speech engines. The way message content is processed depends on the selected engine.

Piper

When Piper is selected, text-to-speech generation is performed locally on the computer running Marionette. Message text sent to Piper is not transmitted to an external text-to-speech service by Marionette.

Fish Audio

When Fish Audio is selected, the text Marionette is instructed to speak is transmitted to Fish Audio's services so that the requested speech can be generated.

5. Temporary Audio Files

Generated audio may be temporarily stored on the computer running Marionette while it is being prepared or played.

These temporary audio files are deleted after they are no longer required by the TTS system.

6. Third-Party Services

Marionette may use third-party services to provide certain functionality. These services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

When Fish Audio is selected as the TTS engine, message text used for speech generation is transmitted to Fish Audio.

7. Data Retention

Marionette is not designed to permanently retain Discord message content.

Temporary information used during message processing and audio playback is removed when it is no longer required.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent users' personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services when necessary to provide a feature, such as text-to-speech generation through Fish Audio.

9. Your Choices

Users who do not wish to have their messages processed by Marionette may avoid interacting with the application or request that a server administrator remove Marionette from the server.

10. Data Requests

If you have questions about information processed by Marionette or believe that information associated with you has been retained and would like to request its deletion, please contact us using the address below.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Marionette's functionality or data practices change, or when applicable requirements change.

The date shown at the beginning of this policy will be updated when material changes are made.
